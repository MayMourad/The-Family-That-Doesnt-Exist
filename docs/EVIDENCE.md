## Known / Inferred / Unknown (added v2.1)
Each claim now shows three labelled layers: **Known** = copied from the cited page; **Inferred** = a cautious interpretation, written by the author and labelled as such (not sourced data); **Unknown** = what the sources do not establish. The Inferred sentences are editorial and should be reviewed by the author before submission. Source records also carry Arabic display text (`ar`), translated by the author.
